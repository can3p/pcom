## Translation

When the site has translation turned on, a logged in reader can ask for a
post or an RSS item to be translated into English, or have posts in chosen
languages translated for them automatically.

- A post is sent for translation only if its author ticked "Allow readers to
  translate this post" in the editor. Drafts are never sent. RSS items, which
  are public already, can always be translated.
- What is sent is the post's subject and text, block by block, including any
  @handles, web addresses and code in it, which are marked to be left as they
  are. The addresses of links are not sent. Nothing else about the reader or
  the author is sent.
- The text goes to the translation service the site is configured with,
  currently Microsoft Azure Translator, which processes it under its own terms.
- The translation is stored and shown to every reader who may read the post
  and asks for it. When the post is edited, it is translated again. When its
  author stops allowing translation or deletes the post, the translation is
  deleted.
- Translations are made by a machine and may contain mistakes. They are always
  marked "Automatically translated", with a link to the original.

The site detects the language of every post and RSS item on its own server,
without sending them anywhere.

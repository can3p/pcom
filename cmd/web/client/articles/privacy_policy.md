## Intro

This is a service in alpha version, please use it on your own risk.

We have no plans to use your data except for serving it to you, however
no guarantees are given with regards to data safety at the moment.

The service administration reserves the right to analyze uploaded content
for purposes of modetaration.

## Grammar checks and style reviews

Grammar checks and style reviews are made by Claude, an external AI model from Anthropic. The subject and body
of a post are sent to Anthropic only when you press Check grammar or Style review in the post editor; nothing
is sent as you type.

Style reviews, including the text they reviewed, are stored with the post and are deleted with it. pcom stores
no grammar-check text, only a record of each request: who made it, for which post, its kind, the model, token
counts, whether it succeeded and when. These records stay when a post is deleted and are deleted with your
account.

## Notes, recordings and photos

Notes are private: only you can see them, and no one else is shown them. pcom stores what you write in a note,
its tags, the source you gave it (a book or article, with its author), the time you captured it and where in
the source it is, if you said so.

A note can also hold photos and a recording:

- **Photos** are stored. Their addresses are not listed anywhere, but anyone who has one can open the photo,
  as with the pictures in a post.
- **Recordings** are stored for you alone; only you can play them back. When you record one, or upload an
  audio file, pcom sends the recording, and nothing else of the note, to the speech-to-text provider this
  site is set up to use. The provider turns it into text, which becomes the note's text. Recordings are sent only
  when you make one, and the provider's own terms say what it keeps.
- **Each transcription** is recorded with its time, the provider, the length of the recording, and whether it
  succeeded. pcom keeps these records to count your daily transcription minutes. A record stays when its note
  is deleted, without a link to the note.

Deleting a note deletes its text, tags, source link and recording. Its photos stop belonging to it, but their
files stay in storage, as the images of deleted posts do; ask the service administration to remove a file.
Text you inserted into a post stays in that post. Your notes are deleted with your account.

API keys are kept only as a hash: pcom cannot show a key again after it is created.

## Email subscriptions without an account

You can subscribe by email to an author's public posts without an account. To do that, pcom stores:

- the address you entered, and the mailbox it delivers to, which pcom uses to count the mails it sends to one mailbox;
- a secret link that opens the page where you see and end your subscriptions, which every post mail and the
  mail with the link to your subscriptions carry;
- your subscriptions: the author, whether you have confirmed, and when you confirmed;
- a record of each confirmation mail and each mail with the link to your subscriptions, kept for about a day so
  that pcom can limit how many it sends to one address;
- a copy of each mail pcom sends you, with the address it was sent to.

The author you subscribe to sees your address, and the date you confirmed, on their controls page. They can
remove you there. They are told when you confirm and when you unsubscribe, and the mail names your address.

A subscription that is not confirmed is deleted after three days, if the link in its confirmation mail has not
been used by then. A confirmed subscription lasts until you end it, the author removes you, or the author's
account is deleted. You can end it from the links in any post mail, or from the page that the link opens, with
Unsubscribe or Unsubscribe from all. If you have lost the link, the Subscriptions page sends it to your address
again while you have a subscription. Ending a subscription deletes it. The copies of mail kept under the last
item above are not deleted when you unsubscribe; to have them deleted, ask the service administration.

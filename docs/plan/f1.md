## F1 — Public post feed on the index page (planned, #146)

The first feature wave: it changes behavior on purpose. For an anonymous
visitor, `/` stops being a landing page (hero, demo video, three selling
points) and becomes the feed of public posts, with its own RSS feed. A
logged in user is still redirected from `/` to `/feed`.

Decided on 2026-10-01 (`docs/open-questions.md`):

- **Q15** is the feed's rule: a post is listed when it is published, its
  visibility is `public` **and** its author's `profile_visibility` is
  `public`. `repo.PublishedPostsByProfile` already filters on all three.
- The index and the RSS feed list the **50** newest posts. No pagination;
  "load more" is a later issue.
- **`/explore` stays for logged in users only.** For an anonymous visitor
  it would show the same list as `/`, so it redirects there (302) and leaves
  the anonymous menu. Logged in users keep it unchanged: it also lists the
  public posts of registered-users profiles.
- The site-wide feed is at **`/rss/public`**, next to `/rss/public/:username`.
- The anonymous menu is **Home · Sign up · Login**. "Why pcom?" and the
  `why.md` article go; `/articles/:id` stays, it serves the privacy policy
  and the terms of service.
- The RSS feed is advertised twice: in the page head
  (`<link rel="alternate">`, through `BasePage.RSSFeed`) and as a visible
  `bi-rss` icon next to the index heading, linking to `/rss/public`.
- With the hero gone, the navbar gets a `bi-github` icon linking to
  `https://github.com/can3p/pcom`, for everyone, logged in or not. The
  footer's GitHub link stays.
- RSS items are dated by `PublishedAt`, not `CreatedAt`: the lists are
  ordered by publication, and a draft published later showed with its draft
  date. This fixes the per-user feeds too.

Tests prove what the feature is for (a visitor finds and reads public
writing, a feed reader subscribes to it), not markup details. What a user
does in a page is a browser test; server rules are E2E tests.

### P0

Owns `pkg/repo/posts.go`, `pkg/service/reading/reading.go` and
`pkg/service/reading/reading_test.go`.

`PublishedPostsByProfile` takes a limit. A new `reading.PublicPosts(ctx,
limit)`, documented as the public feed of Q15, returns the posts built with
`postops.ConstructPost(nil, …)`: no actor, no comments, no actions.
`Explore` is unchanged. One table test pins the rule: profile
visibility (public, registered users, connections) × post visibility ×
published or draft, plus order (newest publication first) and the limit.

### P1

Owns `cmd/web/client/html/index.html`, `cmd/web/client/html/header.html`,
`cmd/web/client/html/header--links.html`, `cmd/web/client/articles/why.md`,
`pkg/web/pages.go`, `pkg/web/pages_reading.go`,
`pkg/web/app/routes_public.go`, `pkg/links/link.go`,
`pkg/links/link_test.go`, `e2e/visibility_test.go`, `e2e/seed_test.go`,
`e2e/browser/layout_test.go` and a new `e2e/browser/index_test.go`.

- `/` for an anonymous visitor calls `PublicPosts(ctx, 50)` and renders the
  posts with the feed's post item (no prompt form, no actions) under a
  heading "Public posts" with the RSS icon. `web.Index` takes the posts and
  sets `RSSFeed` to the new `public_feed` link (`/rss/public`). The hero,
  video and selling points are deleted. An empty instance shows one line
  saying there are no public posts yet.
- Menu: anonymous Home · Sign up (`/signup?attribution=header`) · Login;
  the logged in menu is unchanged. The GitHub icon goes into the navbar in
  `header.html`, outside the collapsing menu's user-dependent part.
- `/explore` redirects an anonymous visitor to `/`; `reading.Explore`
  keeps serving logged in users. Remove `why.md` and its menu entry.
- Tests: `TestVisibility_Index` asserts Q15 on the page (the public
  profile's post is listed; the registered-users and connections profiles'
  public posts, and a draft, are not); `TestVisibility_Explore` keeps its
  logged in assertions and asserts the anonymous redirect to `/`; the
  anonymous explore cases in `seed_test.go` and `layout_test.go` go;
  `TestVisibility_Articles` stops using `why.md`. The browser test
  `index_test.go`: an anonymous visitor on `/` sees the public post, opens
  it, reaches the RSS feed from the icon, and logs in from the menu; the
  sign-up entry reaches the signup form. Tier mid: the rule is pinned by
  P0's service test.

### P2

Owns `pkg/web/app/routes_rss.go`, `pkg/postops/rss/rss.go`,
`pkg/postops/rss/rss_test.go` and a new `e2e/public_feed_test.go`.

`GET /rss/public` serves `PublicPosts(ctx, 50)` titled "Public posts on
<project name>", linking to `/`. `rss.ToFeed` drops its unused `author`
parameter and dates items by `PublishedAt`. The E2E test reads the XML: the
qualifying post is there with its publication date and a link to
`/posts/:id`; the draft and the posts of registered-users and connections
profiles are not.

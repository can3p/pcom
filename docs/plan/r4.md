## R4 — Mailers

Status: planned · Depends on: — · Branch: `claude/zealous-shannon-ieo14d`

Every email becomes a declared definition:

- a typed input;
- a sample factory that touches no database, including the awkward variants
  (no subject, long subject, markup in user content, a linked URL);
- templates: the subject and the text through `text/template`, the HTML through `html/template`;
- a golden file per sample holding the subject, the text and the HTML;
- a development preview page that lists every email rendered from its samples.

Call sites stop building mail bodies with `fmt.Sprintf`, and escaping user content becomes `html/template`'s
job instead of each mail's. All 14 mails are in scope: the 9 in `pkg/mail` and the 5 admin notifications in
`pkg/admin` (new user, new waiting list member, signup confirmed, throwaway email signup, page failure), which
move into `pkg/mail`.

Decided by the owner:

- **Whitespace may change.** The goldens in `pkg/mail/testdata` define "unchanged output" up to whitespace:
  a template may drop the stray tab indentation of today's bodies. `git diff -w` on an existing golden is
  empty, or every remaining hunk is listed in the task's report with its cause (e.g. `html/template`
  normalizing a URL in an `href`), and the coordinator accepts it.
- **The preview lives behind a flag**, `--dev-routes` / `DEV_ROUTES`, off by default and turned on only in
  the local config (`docker-compose.yml`'s app service and `.env.example`). With it off the routes are not
  mounted at all.
- **The job queue is not in R4.** Mail already enters `outgoing_emails` in the same transaction as the change
  that causes it; a general queue mostly pays off for the feed poller and is its own wave, R8. R4 only fixes
  the sender (M6).

### Constraints for every task

- `pkg/mail` exports one envelope type, `mail.Envelope` (M0 folds `Outgoing` into it).
- A mail's public constructor keeps its signature, so its service callers don't change. Only M0 (the
  envelope type) and M4 (the admin mails' new package) edit callers.
- A sample is named after the golden it reproduces: `testdata/<sample>.golden`, so an existing golden is
  rewritten in place and its diff reviewable. New samples add new goldens.
- The golden format is the current `mailsToGolden` one (EmailType, UniqueID, From, To, Subject, TEXT, HTML).
- A per-mail Go test whose cases are now samples is deleted; the `_NotToMyself` tests (no mail for yourself)
  stay. The coordinator `git rm`s test files left empty and the admin goldens M4 replaces.
- `e2e/` is not edited, except the route-table row M5 adds.

### M0 The mail contract

Owns `pkg/mail/mail.go` (new), `pkg/mail/templates/` (new), `pkg/mail/confirm_signup.go`,
`pkg/mail/login_code.go`, `pkg/mail/new_post.go` (only to remove `Outgoing`), `pkg/mail/mail_test.go`,
`pkg/mail/golden_test.go` (new), `pkg/mail/testdata/confirm_signup.golden`,
`pkg/mail/testdata/login_code.golden`, `pkg/service/posts/posts.go`, and the `Outgoing` → `Envelope`
renames in `pkg/mail/*_test.go` and `pkg/service/posts/*.go`.

Define in `pkg/mail/mail.go`:

- `Envelope` (unique id, email type, `*sender.Mail`), replacing `Outgoing` everywhere; `posts.Service.queue`
  and `queueE` take `*mail.Envelope`.
- A declaration of one mail: its email type (the queue's `email_type`), its template name, a typed input,
  and a `Samples` function returning named inputs built from in-memory `model` structs, no database. The
  shape is yours (a generic `Definition[In]` with a `Render(In) (*Envelope, error)` is the expected one);
  the from address, the recipient and the unique id are part of the input or computed from it.
- Templates embedded with `embed.FS` from `pkg/mail/templates/<name>.subject.txt`, `<name>.txt` (both
  `text/template`) and `<name>.html` (`html/template`), parsed once at init; a missing or broken template
  panics at init, not at send time.
- A registry, `mail.All()`, listing every declared mail with its rendered samples, in a stable order. The
  preview page (M5) and the golden test use it; it is the only list of mails.
- `golden_test.go`: one test, `TestMails_Goldens`, that renders every sample of every registered mail into
  `testdata/<sample>.golden` with the `mailsToGolden` format (move the helper here), plus a test that every
  registered mail has at least one sample and every sample name is unique.

Convert LoginCode and ConfirmSignup as the reference conversions: their constructors keep their signatures
and build the input, then render. Their samples are `login_code` and `confirm_signup`; delete
`TestLoginCode` and `TestConfirmSignup` once the goldens reproduce them.

Write the conventions the other tasks follow (where a mail's declaration, samples and templates live, how a
`template.HTML` markdown body enters an input) as the package doc comment of `mail.go`; M1–M4 read it.

### M6 Send each mail in its own transaction

Owns `pkg/mail/sender/dbsender/`, `pkg/repo/mail_queue.go`, `cmd/web/serve.go` (only the `NewSender` line).

Today `sendEmails` locks every pending row in one transaction and calls the real sender while holding it: a
failed status update or commit rolls every mail of the batch back to `new`, and they go out again. Instead,
list the due ids, then for each one: a transaction that locks that row (`FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, skipping it
if another worker has it or it is no longer `new`), sends it and records the outcome. One mail's failure
doesn't touch the others. Replace `attemptsNumber` and `retryIntervals` with an option
(`dbsender.WithRetryIntervals`, default `dbsender.DefaultRetryIntervals` equal to the config default) wired
from `cfg.Mail.RetryIntervals`. Tests: a failing update of one mail leaves the others `sent`; the retry
schedule follows the option; a mail fails after the last interval.

## F16 — Notes attached to a post

Status: planned · Depends on: — · Branch: `feat/f16-post-notes`

An author gathers notes around a post they are writing (one at a time, or every note with a tag) and comes
back to them on the post's own page: the material behind the post sits next to it, without being copied
into its text. Attached notes stay as private as every note: only the author ever sees them, on the post
page, in the editor, nowhere else.

Attaching is not inserting. The post editor's Notes panel inserts a note's text into the body (a `note_post_links`
row with `how = 'inserted'`); attaching adds the note to the post's material and changes no text
(`how = 'attached'`). A note can be both, and can be attached to several posts.

### Behavior

- **Attach one note**: from the note (on `/controls/notes`, "Attach to post…" with a picker of the author's recent
  drafts and posts) and from the post editor's Notes panel ("Attach" next to Insert and Append).
- **Attach a tag**: "Attach all notes tagged X" in the same places. The tag stays attached: the notes
  carrying it now and every note tagged with it later belong to the post (`post_note_tags (post_id, tag)`;
  a note's tags are `tags || text_tags`). The confirmation says so before attaching: "12 notes tagged
  X are attached now, and every note you tag X from now on joins this post." The post's notes show which
  tags are attached, each with Detach. A note attached through a tag can be hidden
  from that post without removing its tag (`note_post_links` with `how = 'excluded'`).
- **On the post page** (`single_post.html`), when the viewer is the post's author, the comments get a
  toggle: **Comments (n) | Notes (n)**, comments selected by default. Notes are loaded with htmx from
  `GET /posts/:id/notes` when the toggle is first switched, so the page itself is built as for any reader
  and nothing about notes is in it for anyone else; readers see the comments as today, without a toggle.
  A draft has no comments, so its page shows the notes at once. The toggle's last choice is kept per
  browser (`localStorage`). Each note shows as on `/controls/notes` (text, photos, audio player, source, tags) with Open, Insert
  into post (opens the editor with the note inserted at the end) and Detach. Grouped by how it came
  (attached one by one, by tag) or by source, as the list's own grouping does, and filtered with the notes' tag
  bar.
- **In the editor** the Notes panel opens on the post's attached notes first, then the rest.
- **On `/controls/notes`** a note shows the posts it is attached to, and the list filters by post.
- **Attaching is not using.** An attached note stays in the notes' "unused" filter until its text is inserted
  into a post.
- **Privacy is the rule this wave is checked against.** The notes section and route exist only for the
  post's author: not for another user who may read the post, not on a share link (`shared_post.html`),
  not in RSS, the zip export, the API's post responses, the feed, or any mail: the comment and connection
  mails, and the mails to email subscribers (F11), which jobs render when they are sent. Detaching, deleting the post or
  deleting the note removes the link and nothing else; a note is never deleted by its post. The
  confirmation of a note's delete names the posts the note is attached to.
- **Turning a post back into a draft or publishing it** doesn't change its notes.

### T0

Migration: `note_post_link_how` gains `'attached'` and `'excluded'`; `post_note_tags (post_id references
posts on delete cascade, tag text, created_at, pk (post_id, tag))`. `pkg/service/notes`: `Attach(actor,
noteID, postID)`, `AttachTag(actor, postID, tag)`, `DetachTag`, `Exclude`, `Detach`, `ForPost(actor, postID)`
(attached ∪ tagged − excluded, newest first) and `PostsOf(actor, noteID)`; the actor must own both the note
and the post, otherwise not found. A privacy table test over every reader of a post (another user with
access, a share link, anonymous on a public post, the RSS and export builders) asserting none of them
reaches a note, and a rendered `posts.published` subscriber mail of a post with attached notes holding none
of their text.

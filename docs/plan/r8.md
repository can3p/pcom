## R8 — Job queue

Status: planned · Depends on: R4 (it rewrites the mail sender) · Branch: `refactor/r8-jobs`

One background worker for everything pcom does outside a request, replacing the two hand-written pollers:
`dbsender.RunPoller` (mail) and the feed poller in `pkg/service/feeds/poller.go`. A job is enqueued in the
same transaction as the state change that causes it, as mail already is through `outgoing_emails`.

What it buys:

- **The feed poller leaves its long transaction.** `refreshFeeds` downloads feeds and images inside the
  transaction that holds the feed row lock, and with `GlobalImageDownloadTimeout` per item that can last
  minutes. As one job per feed, the download runs outside any transaction and only the save is
  transactional.
- **Fan-out leaves the request.** Saving a post renders one mail per connection inside the request's
  transaction (`pkg/service/posts/save.go`); a "post published" job builds them in the worker instead.
- **Retries, backoff and dead jobs become settings and are visible**, instead of per-poller code.
- **Later background work** (exports, media processing) gets a worker without a third poller.

What it costs: a dependency (River fits: pcom already uses pgx v5), a migration, the move of
`outgoing_emails`, and `App.WaitMailSent` / `QueuedMails` in `e2e/harness.go`, the only e2e code that knows
the queue. E2E and browser tests keep asserting on the mail tommy received.

Tasks are planned when the open questions are answered.

### Open questions

- **Library.** River, or a home-grown jobs table generalizing `outgoing_emails`?
- **Render at enqueue or at send?** Rendering in the worker shows the post as it is when the mail goes out
  (an edit in between is included) and lets a template fix reach queued mail; rendering at enqueue keeps
  today's snapshot.
- **Mail history.** Keep a sent-mail record (today's `outgoing_emails` rows with their payload), or rely on
  the queue's finished jobs and tommy / Mailjet for what was delivered?
- **Feed transaction shape (was Q9).** One job per feed with the download outside the transaction, or keep
  the row lock for the whole refresh and only bound its time?

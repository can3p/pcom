## R8 — Job queue

Status: running · Depends on: — · Branch: `claude/vigilant-davinci-h3uooj`

One background worker for everything pcom does outside a request, replacing the two hand-written pollers:
`dbsender.RunPoller` (mail) and the feed poller in `pkg/service/feeds/poller.go`. A job is enqueued in the
same transaction as the state change that causes it, as mail already is through `outgoing_emails`.

What it buys:

- **The feed poller leaves its long transaction.** `refreshFeeds` downloads feeds and images inside the
  transaction that holds the feed row lock, and with `GlobalImageDownloadTimeout` per item that can last
  minutes. As one job per feed, the download runs outside any transaction and only the save is
  transactional.
- **Fan-out leaves the request.** Saving a post renders one mail per connection inside the request's
  transaction (`pkg/service/posts/save.go`); a "post published" job builds them in the worker instead.
- **Retries, backoff and dead jobs are configured and visible in one place**, instead of per-poller code
  (mail has its retry schedule as a setting already; the feed poller has none).
- **Later background work** (exports, media processing) gets a worker without a third poller.

### Decisions (owner, 2026-11-05)

- **Library: River** (`github.com/riverqueue/river`, v0.51.0), through its `riverdatabasesql` driver over
  pcom's `*sql.DB` (opened with pgx's stdlib driver), so a job is inserted on the `*sql.Tx` a service
  already holds. The driver runs River poll-only (no LISTEN), which is enough here.
- **Mail is rendered at send.** A mail job's args hold ids and the values the database doesn't keep (a
  login code, a page failure's error); the worker loads the rows as they are when it runs and calls the
  `pkg/mail` constructor. An edit between enqueue and send is in the mail, and a template fix reaches mail
  already queued.
- **pcom keeps a sent-mail record**: a row per sent mail with its rendered payload, in a new table
  `sent_emails`, unique on `(email_type, unique_id)`. It is also what drops repeats, forever, as
  `outgoing_emails`' constraint does today: River's own uniqueness lasts only as long as finished jobs are
  retained.
- **Feeds: one job per feed, the download outside any transaction.** Only the save is transactional.

### How it is built

The contract L0 builds and every later task uses:

- **`pkg/jobs`** owns River. `jobs.NewInserter(db)` is an insert-only client (no workers) that services get
  through `app.Deps` and the registry; `jobs.NewRunner(db, registry, opts...)` is the working client
  `web serve` starts and stops. Settings (`pkg/config`, group `Jobs`): the fetch poll interval, workers per
  queue, how long finished jobs are kept; mail keeps `--email-retry-intervals`.
- **Enqueueing** goes through `repo`: `type JobQueue interface { Insert(ctx, exec Executor, args JobArgs) error }`
  with `type JobArgs interface{ Kind() string }` (no River import in `repo`), and
  `(*Store).Enqueue(ctx, q JobQueue, args JobArgs) error` on the store's executor, so a job exists only if
  the transaction commits. The `pkg/jobs` inserter uses `InsertTx` when the executor is a `*sql.Tx`.
  Per-kind insert options (max attempts, uniqueness) come from the args' `InsertOpts()` method.
- **Registering work**: `jobs.Registry` collects workers (`jobs.Register`, `jobs.RegisterMail`,
  `jobs.RegisterPeriodic`) and holds the store and delivery mail workers use. Each service that runs jobs
  has `func (s *Service) RegisterJobs(r *jobs.Registry)` and gets its queue with the option
  `WithJobs(repo.JobQueue)` (field `s.jobs`); L0 adds both, empty, to `accounts`, `posts` and `feeds`,
  and `registry.Services.RegisterJobs` calls all three from `web serve`, so the tasks never share that
  file. Services keep their constructors' signatures.
- **Mail jobs.** `jobs.RegisterMail(r, render func(ctx, A) (*mail.Envelope, error))` registers a mail kind `A`.
  Its worker renders, then in one transaction inserts the `sent_emails` row (`ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`; a
  conflict means it was sent, and the job completes without sending) and hands the mail to the delivery
  (`sender.Sender`); a delivery error rolls back and River retries on the `--email-retry-intervals`
  schedule (`NextRetry`), and after one attempt more than there are intervals the job is cancelled, as
  today's mail fails. Mail
  kinds are named `mail.<email_type>`. A render func that finds nothing to send (a deleted post, a
  recipient gone) returns a nil envelope and the job completes.
- **Render funcs** live in the service that owns the data, as `Render<Mail>(ctx, args)` methods reading
  outside any transaction.
- **Tests**: `pkg/testutil/fakequeue` records inserted args (`Inserted()`, and `fakequeue.Of[T](q) []T`);
  service tests assert the args a change enqueued, and test the render funcs against the test database for
  what the mail says. `fakesender` stays as the fake delivery for worker tests.
- **Deploy order.** L0's migrations create River's tables (River's own SQL, exported with `rivermigrate`,
  one sql-migrate file per River version since River runs each in its own transaction, plus its
  `river_migration` rows) and `sent_emails`, copies the sent rows of
  `outgoing_emails` into it, and turns every unsent row into a `mail.rendered` job: a kind whose args are a
  rendered payload, which the worker sends as is; those rows are marked failed so an old poller still
  running during the deploy doesn't send them too. `outgoing_emails` stays during the wave, since the old
  poller keeps it working for mails not yet converted; its drop is L5, a stacked branch.
- **E2E** (`e2e/harness.go`): `WaitMailSent` waits until no `outgoing_emails` row is `new` and no
  `river_job` row of a `mail.%` or `posts.%` kind is unfinished; `QueuedMails` counts both queues.
  L4 removes the `outgoing_emails` half.

### L1 — Account mails as jobs

Owns `pkg/service/accounts/` (code and tests), the `SendAdminMail` call in `pkg/web/app/router.go`, and
the tests in `pkg/forms/` (but `form_post_new_test.go`, which is L2's) and `pkg/web/` that assert on account mail. Every mail the accounts service
queues (`invites.go`, `signup.go`, `codes.go`, `accounts.go`'s `send`/`SendAdminMail`/`notifyAdmin`)
becomes `tx.Enqueue` of a `mail.<email_type>` args struct (ids plus what the database doesn't hold: a
login code, a page failure's error and request) and a `Render…` method registered in `RegisterJobs`. The
mail each renders is the one sent today: the render tests reproduce the old assertions on subject, body and
recipients. The service stops using `repo.MailQueue`.

### L2 — Post mails and the post-published fan-out as jobs

Owns `pkg/service/posts/` (code and tests) and `pkg/forms/form_post_new_test.go`. Prompt, prompt answer and comment mails become mail jobs as in
L1. Saving a published post no longer renders mail: it enqueues one `posts.published` job (args: post id),
unique by post id; its worker loads the post's connections and inserts one `mail.new_post` job per
recipient with today's unique id, so a re-run sends nothing twice. A test proves the mail shows the post as
edited after enqueue. The service stops using `repo.MailQueue`.

### L3 — Feed refresh as one job per feed

Owns `pkg/service/feeds/` and the feed queries in `pkg/repo/feeds.go`. A periodic `feeds.schedule` job,
every `--rss-poll-interval` (new setting, default 10s, replacing `pollEvery`), inserts one `feeds.refresh`
job (args: feed id) per due feed, unique by feed id among unfinished jobs. The refresh worker:

1. outside any transaction: loads the feed, fetches it, finds the new items with read-only queries,
   converts each to markdown and downloads and stores its images (`media.StoreUpload` on the plain store);
2. in one transaction: locks the feed row (`FOR UPDATE`), re-reads which items exist (another refresh may
   have saved some), inserts the new items and the subscribers' rows, and saves the feed's counters and
   `next_fetch_at`.

A fetch error is saved on the feed as today and the job completes: the feed's own `next_fetch_at` is its
backoff. Images stored for an item another refresh saved first are left as they are (as a failed refresh
leaves them today). `RunPoller`, `refreshFeeds` and `LockFeed`'s `SKIP LOCKED` go. The poller tests keep
their assertions against the worker; a new test proves no transaction is open during the download (the
fetcher fake checks it can update the feed row from another connection).

### L4 — Remove the old queue code; docs

Owns `pkg/mail/sender/dbsender/` (deleted), `repo.MailQueue` and `pkg/repo/mail_queue.go`, the
`OutgoingEmail` factory helpers, `fakesender`'s `Send`, `app.Deps.Sender` and the registry field,
`--email-poll-interval` and the help golden, the `outgoing_emails` half of the E2E helpers, and the docs:
`pkg/mail/mail.go`'s package doc, `docs/architecture.md` (a "Background jobs" section: how to add a job
kind), `docs/testing.md` (fakequeue). `model.OutgoingEmail` stays until L5.

### L5 — Drop `outgoing_emails`

A migration on a branch stacked on the wave, merged after the wave is deployed: first turns any row
still `new` into a `mail.rendered` job as L0's migration did (an old machine may have queued one during the
deploy), then drops the table and its enum, and `model.OutgoingEmail`.

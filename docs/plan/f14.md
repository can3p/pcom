## F14 — Mail through Brevo

Status: planned · Depends on: an owner's Brevo account and the facts in "Brevo facts" · Branch: `feat/f14-brevo`

pcom sends through Brevo instead of Mailjet, so that one provider both sends and receives mail: F13
receives replies through Brevo's inbound parsing, and F12 reads bounces and complaints from Brevo's
webhooks. Mailjet's inbound parsing doesn't fit F13 (see "Provider facts" in `docs/plan/f13.md`).

Decided on 2026-11-05:

- **Brevo replaces Mailjet** for transactional sending (its transactional email API), inbound parsing (F13)
  and delivery events (F12). pcom never runs two providers for long: Mailjet's sender stays only as the
  fallback until the cutover has run in production, then a separate PR removes it.
- **The provider is a setting.** `MAIL_PROVIDER` (`mailjet` or `brevo`) chooses the sender, each with its
  own config group (`MJ_*` as now, `BREVO_API_KEY` and `BREVO_API_BASE`). Rolling back is changing one
  setting. Nothing outside `cmd/web/serve.go` and `pkg/config` knows which one runs.
- **pcom's mail rules hold on Brevo.** Mails have no tracking and no images of their own
  (`docs/product.md`, Interface). So Brevo's open and click tracking must be off for pcom's mail, and the free
  plan must not add Brevo branding to transactional mail. If either can't be guaranteed, the wave stops and
  the owner decides.
- **The Brevo sender carries what F11 to F13 need:** custom headers (F11's `List-Unsubscribe` and
  `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, F13's `Auto-Submitted`), Reply-To (F13), and the provider's message id returned
  from `Send` (F12 matches complaints to mails by it).
- **The test reply answers F13's Q-F.** During the cutover, the owner sends a reply from a Gmail address to
  the Brevo receiving subdomain. The webhook's `Headers` then show whether Brevo adds its own
  `Authentication-Results`.

### Brevo facts

Filled in by TV from Brevo's own pages, with a source for each point. "Not documented" means the pages say
nothing on the point; "unverified" means the page could not be read.

| Point | Brevo | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Send API endpoint and auth | `POST https://api.brevo.com/v3/smtp/email`, API key in the `api-key` header (OAuth 2.0 client credentials also accepted) | [send reference](https://developers.brevo.com/reference/send-transac-email) |
| Custom headers per message | `headers`, a map: "Custom email headers (non-standard headers)"; names are converted to Title-Case; "Standard email headers are not supported". Whether `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Auto-Submitted` count as standard is not documented | [send reference](https://developers.brevo.com/reference/send-transac-email) |
| Reply-To | `replyTo` (`email` required, `name` optional), per message and per `messageVersions` entry | [send reference](https://developers.brevo.com/reference/send-transac-email) |
| Message id in the send response | 201 with `messageId`, e.g. `<201798300811.5787683@relay.domain.com>` (with angle brackets), or `messageIds` for versions | [send reference](https://developers.brevo.com/reference/send-transac-email) |
| Turning off open and click tracking for transactional mail | Not available on a free plan. The only per-message switch is `removeTrackingPixel`, "Only available to Enterprise plans: for other accounts the field is accepted but ignored", and with it "Click tracking is unchanged". `contactPixelTrackingConsent: false` anonymises opens and clicks but keeps the pixel and tracked links. No account setting that turns tracking off is documented on the readable pages; the help center (unreadable: it answers automated requests with a bot check) is reported by search results to offer anonymisation only | [send reference](https://developers.brevo.com/reference/send-transac-email), [anonymise tracking](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/11643306229906) (unverified) |
| Branding on free-plan transactional mail | The pricing page lists "No Brevo logo: Remove the 'Sent with Brevo' footer" as a feature of the paid plans, not of Free. Whether the footer is added to API transactional mail is not stated on the readable pages; the help center is reported by search results to say Free mail always carries it (unverified) | [pricing](https://www.brevo.com/pricing/), [free plan limits](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/208580669) (unverified) |
| Free plan: daily quota, whether inbound counts toward it | "up to 300 emails per day" once the account is approved for sending. Whether inbound mail counts is not documented | [pricing](https://www.brevo.com/pricing/) |
| Sending-domain authentication (DNS records) | TXT `brevo-code:<code>` on the domain, DKIM TXT at `mail._domainkey`, DMARC TXT at `_dmarc` (e.g. `v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=...`); checked with `GET /v3/senders/domains/{domain}`, authenticated with `PUT .../authenticate` | [domain authentication](https://developers.brevo.com/docs/domain-authentication-and-verification) |
| Inbound parsing: MX hosts, catch-all, payload fields, webhook auth, retries, size limit | MX 10 `inbound1.sendinblue.com.`, MX 20 `inbound2.sendinblue.com.` on a receiving subdomain that differs from the sending domain; catch-all (`*@reply.yourdomain.com`); payload `items[]` with `Uuid`, `MessageId`, `InReplyTo`, `From`, `To`, `Recipients`, `Cc`, `ReplyTo`, `SentAtDate`, `Subject`, `RawHtmlBody`, `RawTextBody`, `ExtractedMarkdownMessage`, `ExtractedMarkdownSignature`, `SpamScore`, `Attachments` (download tokens), `Headers`; webhook auth by basic auth in the URL, a bearer token, custom headers or Brevo's IP ranges; retries as for delivery events below; size limit not documented; authentication results not documented (F13's Q-F test reply) | [inbound parsing](https://developers.brevo.com/docs/inbound-parse-webhooks), [secure webhooks](https://developers.brevo.com/docs/secured-webhooks), [retries](https://developers.brevo.com/docs/retry-mechanism) |
| Delivery event webhooks: hard bounce and complaint event names and fields, auth, retries | Subscribed as `hardBounce` and `spam` (type `transactional`); the payload's `event` is `"hard_bounce"` or `"spam"`. Fields: `event`, `email`, `id` (webhook id), `date`, `ts`, `message-id` (without angle brackets, unlike the send response), `ts_event`, `ts_epoch`, `subject` (hard bounce), `reason` (hard bounce), `X-Mailin-custom`, `tags`, `sending_ip`, `template_id`, `source`. Auth as for inbound. Retries: 4 after the first attempt, at 10 min, 1 h, 2 h and 8 h; any 4xx except 429 and any 5xx stops them. At most 40 webhooks per account | [transactional webhooks](https://developers.brevo.com/docs/transactional-webhooks), [create webhook](https://developers.brevo.com/reference/create-webhook), [retries](https://developers.brevo.com/docs/retry-mechanism), [webhooks](https://developers.brevo.com/docs/how-to-use-webhooks) |
| API rate limits | General tier (all plans): `POST /v3/smtp/email` 3,600,000 an hour and 1,000 a second; other `/v3/smtp/...` endpoints 300 an hour; all other endpoints 100 an hour. Over the limit: `429 Too Many Requests` | [rate limits](https://developers.brevo.com/docs/api-limits) |

Both stopping conditions hold on what Brevo documents. Open and click tracking can't be turned off on a
free plan: the API removes only the open pixel, only on Enterprise, and leaves click tracking on. The
pricing page sells the removal of the "Sent with Brevo" footer as a paid-plan feature; whether it applies
to transactional mail is unverified.

### Open questions

- **Q-T. Brevo's tracking and branding (from TV).** pcom's mail must have no tracking and no provider
  branding, and Brevo's free plan can't guarantee either: click tracking stays on except, for the open
  pixel only, on Enterprise, and the free plan appears to add a "Sent with Brevo" footer. The owner
  decides whether to keep F14 on Brevo (accepting tracking and the footer, or paying for a plan without
  the footer, which still leaves tracking on), or to go back to Q-B's options in `docs/plan/f13.md`.
  Before deciding, one test mail from a free Brevo account shows whether the footer and the tracked links
  appear on API mail.
- **Q-H. Custom headers.** Brevo's send API says "Standard email headers are not supported" in
  `headers`. The same test mail should carry `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and
  `Auto-Submitted`, to check they arrive (F11, F13).

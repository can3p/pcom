# R8 — Job queue (done 2026-11-05, branch `claude/vigilant-davinci-h3uooj`, cleanup #273)

**Built.** One background worker for everything pcom does outside a request, on River (v0.51.0) over the
existing `*sql.DB` through `riverdatabasesql` (poll-only):

- `pkg/jobs`: an insert-only client services get as `repo.JobQueue` (`WithJobs`, `tx.Enqueue`), a
  `Registry` (`Register`, `RegisterMail`, `RegisterPeriodic`) every service fills in its `RegisterJobs`, and
  the `Runner` `web serve` starts and stops. Settings `--jobs-poll-interval`, `--jobs-workers`,
  `--jobs-retention`; River logs through the new `logging.Slog`.
- Mail is rendered at send: account mails (L1) and post mails (L2) enqueue ids plus what the database
  doesn't keep, and `Render<Mail>` methods rebuild the envelope with the same `pkg/mail` constructors when the
  job runs. The worker records the mail in the new `sent_emails` table and delivers it in one transaction.
  Publishing a post enqueues one `posts.published` job whose worker fans out one `mail.post_notification`
  per reader.
- Feeds (L3): a periodic `feeds.schedule` job enqueues one `feeds.refresh` per due feed (unique per feed);
  the refresh downloads the feed and its images outside any transaction and saves in one transaction that
  locks the feed row and re-checks existing items. `--rss-poll-interval` replaces the poller's constant.
- L4 removed `dbsender`, `repo.MailQueue` and its queries, the `OutgoingEmail` factories,
  `--email-poll-interval`, and the mail-queue parameter of `accounts.New` and `posts.New`.
- Migrations: River's schema as one file per River version, and `sent_emails`, which took the sent rows of
  `outgoing_emails` while every unsent row became a `mail.rendered` job (a pre-rendered mail sent as is).

**Decisions (owner, at the start).** River over a home-grown table generalizing `outgoing_emails` (retries,
backoff, uniqueness and dead jobs come with it; pcom already used pgx v5). Render at send over today's
snapshot at enqueue (an edit before sending is mailed; a template fix reaches queued mail). Keep a sent-mail
record (`sent_emails`) rather than rely on River's finished jobs, which are pruned; it is also the
permanent dedup. One job per feed with the download outside the transaction, over keeping the row lock and
bounding its time (open question Q9).

**What turned out wrong.**
- River's version 004 adds an enum value and uses it in one migration; sql-migrate runs a file as one
  transaction, so the first export (all versions in one file) failed. One file per version fixed it.
- Render at send made two audit findings behavior bugs, not test gaps: a post made draft before its fan-out
  ran was still mailed, and a reader who lost the connection between enqueue and send still got the mail.
  Workers now re-check visibility at run time (rule added to `docs/architecture.md`).
- The feed refresh lost its logger: `serve.go` had been the only caller of `feeds.WithLogger`. The server
  logger now reaches services through `registry.Deps.Logger`.
- Assertion audits of all three conversions found render tests without subjects or recipients, dedup claims
  nothing tested (fakequeue ignores insert options), and a lock test that hung to the `go test` timeout under
  mutation instead of failing. All fixed before merge.
- L1 and L2 were planned as parallel, but the `pkg/forms` tests of accounts and posts share helpers; they
  ran in sequence. L3's agent couldn't be resumed for its audit fixes because its worktree had been removed;
  a fresh agent did them.

**Left out.** Dropping `outgoing_emails`, `model.OutgoingEmail` and, later, the `mail.rendered` kind waits
for the deploy (an old machine may queue a row during it): #273. The session cleanup and login pruner stay
goroutines; they could become periodic jobs. Throwaway-address and page-failure admin mails get a fresh
unique id at render, as their constructors make one; a delivery that succeeds but whose record fails to
commit is resent, as with the old queue.

**Docs changed.** `docs/architecture.md` (Background jobs: why River, render at send, `sent_emails`, feed
refresh shape, re-check at run time; `logging.Slog`), `docs/product.md` (a mail shows what is true when it
goes out), `docs/testing.md` (fakequeue, fakesender as delivery, uniqueness on a real inserter, fail-fast
lock tests), `docs/running.md`, `docs/gogo-extraction.md`, `pkg/mail/mail.go`'s package doc,
`pkg/feedops/AGENTS.md`, `docs/implementation-plan.md` (shared test helpers make tasks sequential), the
`wave-run` skill (keep a worktree until its audit fixes are in).

**Cost.** 1 coordinator session (170 turns, peak 286k context, 175k of tool results; flags: `cat`×9 and raw
builds×4 in the coordinator, `cat`×26 and raw builds×5 across the wave) and 9 subagents: the pkg/jobs tests
(mid), L1 mid (resumed twice: rate limit, audit fixes), L2 mid (resumed once), L3 strong in a worktree
(resumed twice), a fresh strong agent for L3's audit fixes, L4 mid, and 3 strong read-only audits. Skills:
wave-run×1, wave-close×1. Far more than F10 (67 turns, peak 157k): the coordinator built the L0 contract
itself, and a usage limit plus three rounds of audit fixes stretched the session; the audits paid for
themselves with two real bugs.

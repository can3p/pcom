# F11 — Email subscriptions to an author's public posts (done 2026-11-06, branch `claude/vibrant-fermat-dxl7k9`)

**Built.** A visitor without an account subscribes by email to an author's public posts, confirms from a mail,
and manages every subscription of that address from a link in each post mail. The author opts in under the new
Audience section of Settings, sees confirmed addresses on the controls page with Remove, and is mailed when a
subscription is confirmed and when a subscriber leaves. The post form and the API offer to mail a public post
to subscribers once (`posts.subscribers_mailed_at`).

- `pkg/emailaddr` (TD) is the one importer of `can3p/anti-disposable-email`, now v0.1.0: canonical mailbox
  (the library's providers plus pcom's `+tag` removal on every domain), disposable and relay flags, and a DNS
  check (`EMAIL_MX_TIMEOUT`) that subscribing uses and signup doesn't. Signup now accepts relays and refuses
  disposable subdomains.
- `mail.Audience` (TG) replaced `NoAccount` with a third, subscriber audience; post mails to subscribers
  carry `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) through gogo v0.4.1's `sender.Mail.Headers`.
- `pkg/service/subscriptions` (T0, T1, T1b): subscribe, confirm, manage, unsubscribe, lost link, caps per
  canonical mailbox from `subscription_mail_log`, the site-wide brake with one admin mail an hour, the pruner,
  and the fan-out of a public post as one event job whose worker re-checks the post and the author.
- `pkg/abuse` (TA): a signed proof-of-work challenge solved by the `pow` Stimulus controller with
  `crypto.subtle`, a honeypot and a minimum fill time; replays are refused through `pow_challenges_used`.
- Routes and pages (T3), the post form (T2), Audience and the controls section (T4), product rules, privacy
  policy, a guide page and screenshots (T5).

**Turned out wrong.**
- R8 (River jobs, mail rendered at send) merged mid-wave. TG's header test in `dbsender` had no home any more,
  and T1's service, written against `outgoing_emails`, no longer compiled; a strong task (T1b) moved it onto
  jobs and the F11 migrations were renamed after master's newest. T2 and T3 had hit a rate limit before
  writing anything, so they were simply re-dispatched on the rebased branch.
- The planned proof-of-work default of 18 bits took about 9.5s at 4× CPU throttling; it is 15 bits (about a
  second).
- An empty nonce passed a 4-bit E2E challenge one time in sixteen (a flaky test); `Verify` now refuses it.
- T3 left the confirm POST and unsubscribe-all CSRF-exempt along with the one-click route; the audit caught it.
- T2 built a private `subscriptions.Service` inside posts; it is injected from the registry instead.
- The controls page's Remove looked grey: `.acts button` outranked `.btn-danger`. A test that asserted the
  class passed regardless; it now compares the computed color with `--err`.
- The docs fact-check found six claims the code didn't back (what the canonical mailbox counts, which mails
  carry the manage link, who calls `Verify`, the no-account footer's mails, logged-in visitors); fixed.

**Left out.** Bounces and complaints (F12, re-planned around the mail worker). `sent_emails` keeps a copy of
every mail, subscriber addresses included, with no pruning; the privacy policy says so. The E2E one-click test
reads the URL from the mail footer, because the test mail server doesn't record headers.

**Decisions taken in the wave.** Confirming still works while an author is paused (the author is told; nothing
is mailed). An expired or unknown confirm link names no author. Ticking "Email this post" mails a published
public post never mailed; autosave never mails. Narrowing the journal switches the setting off and keeps the
subscriptions. The footer's Unsubscribe link opens the manage page on GET and unsubscribes on POST.
`NotifyPost` marks a post only when there is somebody to mail.

**Docs and skills changed at close.** `docs/architecture.md`: services that call services take them through
an option from the registry; the abuse guard paragraph describes the real call path. `docs/implementation-plan.md`
("Planning a wave"): plan against the plumbing another wave is rewriting. `wave-run`: read the owned-files line
back, since `task_prompt.py` pastes "see TX" or a fragment of a bulleted list. `pkg/mail` package doc: the two
hand-kept test tables a new mail joins. `docs/plan/f12.md`: no longer depends on F11, and notes what R8 changed.

T1, T1b, T2 strong; TA, T3, T4 mid; TM, T5 cheap, both resumed once), 2 first T2/T3 runs lost to a rate limit,
3 fresh fix agents (TA tests, T3 CSRF and tests, T4 tests), 5 strong read-only audits and the docs fact-check. Skills: wave-run×1, wave-close×1,
frontend-htmx in 2 subagents (both longer than the median: 55 turns against 22). Heavier than R8 (170 turns,

largest waste and says the "never cat a whole file" preamble line isn't followed by cheap and mid agents.
